This document details the pricing, features, and exclusions for the Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) plans.

---

## 1. Basic Care
*   **Price:** ₹2,499 / year
*   **Target Audience:** Cost-conscious users who want basic coverage.
*   **Description:** "Essential maintenance for your purifier."
*   **Visual Key:** Blue Theme
*   **Included Features:**
    *   ✓ 2 Preventive Maintenance Visits
    *   ✓ Unlimited Breakdown Support
    *   ✓ 10% Discount on Spares
    *   ✓ Priority Service Booking
*   **Exclusions (Not Included):**
    *   ✗ Consumables (Filters/Membrane)
    *   ✗ Electrical Parts
